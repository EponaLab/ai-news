**Key policy:** Customers who grant external agents access assume full liability; banks' own AI agents exempt from additional liability requirements.

**Market impact:** Five major US banks representing approximately **$9.6 trillion in consolidated assets** have now adopted this policy. ### 11. **Two Riemann Hypothesis Proofs Claimed in One Day — Mathematics' Verification Crisis**

In an extraordinary Saturday development, **two separate claims** to prove mathematics' most famous open problem emerged within hours on November 20:

**AI-Assisted Claim (Levent Alpöge + Claude):**
- Anthropic mathematician posted **175,187 Lean 4 files** (17.4 million lines of code) claiming proof of Generalized Riemann Hypothesis
- MIT's Andrew Sutherland independently compiled the Lean code—confirms it compiles using only three standard axioms
- Mathematician Elliot Glazer verified statement is indeed GRH, estimates <1% probability of Lean bug
- **The unprecedented aspect:** Proof is machine-checkable but almost unreadable by humans—Terence Tao admitted "I have only read statements of declarations"

**Human Claim (Alessandro Masullo):**
- Hours after Alpöge's announcement, AMS President Ravi Vakil rushed out rival draft
- Conventional all-human paper, 12 years of work
- Stanford PhD under Brian Conrad, who scrutinized it for months
- Released early specifically because AI claim had landed

**Neither proof is yet peer-reviewed or accepted.** Clay Institute's $1M prize requires broad acceptance plus two years of scrutiny. This is the sharpest test yet of whether formal verification can substitute for human comprehension—the first plausible claim on mathematics' most famous open problem arrives as code that compiles but almost nobody can read.

**Market & Scientific Impact:** The mathematical community is improvising a review process in real time for two claims it did not expect on the same morning. Represents potential inflection point where AI-generated formal proofs outpace human verification capacity.
