### **The Interpretability Breakthrough**

**Goodfire's 90% single-concept neurons:**

**Old belief:** Neural networks are "black boxes" with polysemantic neurons (one neuron doing multiple unrelated jobs)

**New evidence (Qwen3-32B audit):**
- ~90% of neurons have one primary function
- ~7% have two related jobs
- Only 2-4% unexplained

**Why previous interpretability research failed:**
1. Tooling limitations (couldn't isolate individual neuron contributions)
2. Pre-residual architectures (mixed signals across network)
3. Older model designs without LayerNorm

**Modern designs:** Residual connections + LayerNorm push interpretable neuron rates from ~5% to 99-100%

**Implications:**
1. **Model auditing becomes tractable:** Can verify what individual neurons learned
2. **Targeted unlearning:** Remove specific capabilities without retraining
3. **Safety verification:** Check for hazardous knowledge in specific neurons
4. **Regulatory compliance:** "Explainable AI" requirements become achievable

**Strategic value:** Organizations that master interpretability tools can:
- Debug model misbehavior faster
- Prove compliance with interpretable models
- Build trust through transparency
