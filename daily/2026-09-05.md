## 💡 Insights & Analysis

### The Agent Security Gap Widens
The emergence of AIR and HiddenLayer with $150M combined funding signals that **agent supply chain security** is now a critical enterprise concern. As agents integrate third-party skills, MCP servers, and autonomous tool-calling, the attack surface expands dramatically. The market is transitioning from "can we build agents?" to "can we secure them in production?"

### September 2026: From Single-Prompt to Multi-System Agents
This month marks a clear shift from productivity tools to agents that **operate across multiple business systems**. The SoundHound-LivePerson merger, Proofpoint's SOC agent, and the broader agent framework maturation (Microsoft 1.17.0, stable APIs) indicate we're entering the "orchestration era" where agents coordinate across enterprise boundaries rather than operate in isolation.

### GPT-6 Astra Sets New Baseline
With GPT-6 Astra reaching public availability today, the competitive bar for frontier models rises substantially. The 1M context window, $10/$50 pricing, and cyber-tuned variants (via Daybreak) suggest 2026's second half will focus on **specialized agent capabilities** over general intelligence gains.

### The True Agent Reality Check
Menlo Ventures' data revealing that only 16-27% of deployments are "true agents" (with plan-observe-adapt loops) exposes the gap between marketing and implementation. Most production systems remain **routing-based workflows** wrapped around single model calls—a sobering reality check for the agentic AI narrative.
