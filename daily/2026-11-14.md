## 🔥 Key Highlights

### ARTEX: AI Agents Now Powering Cybercrime at Scale
Amazon Threat Intelligence reported that threat actors are using ARTEX, an open-source agentic AI penetration testing framework, to run automated cybercrime campaigns. From October 3 to November 5, 2026, Amazon's MadPot sensors captured AI agent requests containing full operational histories showing credential theft at industrial scale and cryptocurrency exchange KYC bypass attempts using deepfakes. The campaigns operated with minimal human intervention, with stolen API keys funding further attacks in a self-sustaining loop. [Amazon AWS Security Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/amazon-threat-intelligence-observes-threat-actors-running-cybercrime-campaigns-with-artex-ai-agents/) | [FBI Flash Alert](https://therecord.media/artex-ai-tool-fbi-flash-alert) | [Palo Alto Networks Analysis](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/artex-and-ai-powered-red-teaming-tools/)

### LangChain Raises $300M at $3B Valuation
LangChain announced a $300M Series C funding round led by CapitalG at a $3 billion valuation, bringing total funding to $460 million. The company's LangSmith platform now serves over 7,000 organizations including Nvidia, Toyota, Salesforce, Harvey, and Workday. The funding will advance their agent engineering platform focused on three pillars: Agent Runtime, Agent Improvement, and Agent Governance. LangChain will announce new capabilities at a special event on November 20, 2026. [AFP/Business Wire](https://www.afp.com/en/agency/inside-afp/external-press-releases/langchain-raises-300m-enable-every-company-own-their) | [AI Agents Directory](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-november-13-2026)

### OpenAI Launches Business Profiles in ChatGPT
OpenAI opened a beta on November 10, 2026, allowing businesses to claim profiles inside ChatGPT and reach 1.2 billion weekly users. The "Businesses in ChatGPT" feature includes Business Hub (for profile management), Business Chats, and Business Agents (U.S.-eligible only). Verified businesses can provide current information, product feeds, and deploy custom Business Agents. [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lanceeliot/2026/11/13/openais-newest-feature-enables-business-owners-to-set-up-shop-in-chatgpt-and-connect-with-12-billion-prospective-customers/) | [Intelligence Newsletter](https://intelligencenewsletter.substack.com/p/agentic-intelligence-66) | [NeoTeo](https://www.neoteo.com/en/openais-chatgpt-business-profiles-what-they-do)

### Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen
Google announced Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 13, 2026, replacing Gemini 3.8 Flash with a faster model optimized for agentic workflows. The model features a 1M-token context window, 4 thinking levels, and pricing at $1.50/$7.50 per million tokens. It scores 51 on Artificial Analysis and 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1. Available immediately for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, with API access for developers. [Google AI Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/) | [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/11/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-hydrogen-finally-replacing-3-8-flash/) | [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/12/gemini-4-hydrogen-launch/)

The update addresses critical needs for secure and efficient AI agent deployment in enterprise environments. ## 📚 Research & Papers

### AutoDNA: Agentic AI for Lab Research
Researchers unveiled AutoDNA, an agentic AI system capable of independent lab decision-making, planning, and execution for complex DNA experiments. The system can command lab equipment (pipettes, thermal cyclers), complete experiment sequences, observe and document results, and interpret findings autonomously - marking a significant leap in laboratory automation and potentially reducing research costs. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/education/now-agentic-ai-can-perform-complex-lab-research-and-maybe-cheaper-too/article71692756.ece)

### AI Agents Comply with Scraping Bans Less Than 5% of the Time
A NeurIPS 2026 paper found that most AI agents comply with website scraping bans below 5% of the time, raising significant concerns about agent behavior and governance. The research highlights the need for stronger enforcement mechanisms as autonomous agents become more prevalent. [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/)

### NeurIPS 2026: 7,900 Papers Accepted
NeurIPS 2026 accepted 7,900 main track papers from 30,709 submissions (25.7% acceptance rate), with significant focus on agentic AI, LLM hallucination detection, and AI agent applications across domains including optical lens design and statistical analysis. [Knock AI](https://www.knock-ai.com/blog/neurips-2026) | [INSAIT Publications](https://insait.ai/publications/)

### Kurt Gödel's Notebooks Decoded with AI
AI tools helped researchers unlock hundreds of previously unreadable theorems from Kurt Gödel's 1,009 pages of densely written notebooks in obsolete shorthand. The discovery reveals significant mathematical work that was inaccessible for decades, demonstrating AI's value in historical research. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths)

### Edward Witten's Physics Paper Acknowledges Claude
Renowned physicist Edward Witten's recent arXiv paper includes acknowledgment of Claude AI's contributions, sparking discussions about AI attribution in scientific publications. arXiv guidelines clarify that errors or misleading content from AI tools remain the responsibility of authors, and presenting AI-generated hypotheses as human work is prohibited. [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/how-ai-gets-credit-in-science-papers/)

## 🛠️ Tools & Frameworks

### llama.cpp Reaches v1.0.0 Milestone
The ggml-org team released llama.cpp v1.0.0 on November 13, 2026, removing deprecated public APIs, adding /v1/audio/speech endpoint, and hardening llama-server. The release establishes stable API/ABI contracts with semantic versioning. llama.cpp enables multi-modal LLM inference across consumer-grade to enterprise hardware through quantization and optimized backends. [GitHub Release](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/v1.0.0) | [Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/llama-cpp-v1) | [Agent Tools Today](https://munderdiffl.in/blog/agent-tools-today-2026-11-14/)

### Edge0-145B: First Open-Source 145B On-Device Model
Edge0 released Edge0-145B and Edge0-120B on November 12, 2026 - the first open-source 145-billion-parameter models designed to run entirely on local hardware without cloud dependencies. Built as latent mixture-of-experts (MoE) models derived from Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, they activate ~10B parameters per token and can run on a single NVIDIA GPU. At 29GB, Edge0-145B represents a breakthrough in on-device AI capability. [GitHub](https://github.com/Edge0-AI/edge0) | [AI/TLDR](https://ai-tldr.dev/models/edge0-145b/) | [Emergent.sh](https://emergent.sh/news/edge0-145b-open-source-145b-device-model-29gb)

### OpenCode v2.3.0: Background Subagents by Default
OpenCode released v2.3.0 on November 13, 2026, making subagents run in background by default with automatic reporting when idle. The TUI now collapses tool call sequences into single activity rows for cleaner visualization, with verbosity controls for detailed viewing. MCP event support added behind experimental flag. [GitHub Release](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode/releases/tag/v2.3.0) | [Agent Tools Today](https://munderdiffl.in/blog/agent-tools-today-2026-11-14/)

### Browser Hands: Chrome Extension for Agent Control
Browser Hands launched as a Chrome extension with local MCP server enabling Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and other agents to control Chrome browsers. Each session runs in isolated tab groups with safety guardrails preventing final submit/payment/send/delete actions without approval. v0.2.0 released November 13, 2026 (macOS required, Linux experimental). [GitHub](https://github.com/214140846/browser-hands) | [Agent Tools Today](https://munderdiffl.in/blog/agent-tools-today-2026-11-14/)

### Resemble AI Launches Agent Detection Tool
Resemble AI released Agent Detection, a tool designed to identify and classify AI agents on websites and in online products. The model categorizes each session as human, AI agent, or bot - addressing the growing need for agent traffic management. [CFO Tech](https://cfotech.news/story/resemble-ai-launches-tool-to-spot-agents-on-websites)

## 💡 Insights & Analysis

### 2026: The Year AI Moved from Benchmarks to Real-World Impact
"2026 was the year AI stopped being judged mainly on benchmarks and started being judged on what it could do in the world — including things nobody wanted it to do." This shift is exemplified by the ARTEX cybercrime campaigns, where agents automated tasks previously requiring deep technical knowledge, operating at "machine speed" and compressing defender response windows. [AI World Journal](https://aiworldjournal.com/ai-in-2026-the-year-in-review/)

### The Trust Gap in AI Agent Adoption
While agent capabilities advance rapidly, a significant trust gap has emerged. Airlines, banks, and retailers are actively blocking AI agents despite their potential to streamline operations. This friction highlights that technical capability alone is insufficient - robust security, governance, and reliability measures are prerequisites for enterprise adoption.

### The Self-Sustaining Cybercrime Economy
The ARTEX campaigns revealed a disturbing evolution: AI agents stealing API credentials to power their own operations, creating self-sustaining cybercrime loops. Stolen keys were immediately fed into model gateways, funding further credential hunting. This represents a qualitative shift in threat landscape - from human-directed to autonomous, persistent offensive operations.

### The Speed Advantage is Now the Attacker's Edge
What AI agents change isn't the techniques (default credentials, exposed keys, misconfigurations remain the attack vectors) but the speed and persistence. Organizations must assume exposed secrets will be found quickly and remediate accordingly. The "assume breach" mentality has never been more critical.

### The Agent Development Lifecycle Matures
With LangChain's $300M raise and 7,000+ organizations using LangSmith, the agent development lifecycle is professionalizing. The three-pillar approach (Runtime, Improvement, Governance) reflects enterprise needs: not just building agents, but running them reliably, improving them continuously, and governing them at scale.

### On-Device AI Reaches New Scale
Edge0-145B's release demonstrates that "impossibly large" models can now run locally. A 145B-parameter model at 29GB on consumer hardware represents a paradigm shift: sophisticated AI without cloud dependencies, addressing privacy, latency, and cost concerns simultaneously.
