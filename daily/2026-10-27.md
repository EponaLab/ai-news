### **Scientific AI Claims and Verification Crisis**

OpenAI's claims about progress on Kakeya conjecture, ETH, and quantum chromodynamics raise fundamental question: **Can academic verification keep pace with AI claim generation?**

**The Verification Problem:**
- **Generation Speed:** AI can produce thousands of candidate proofs
- **Verification Speed:** Humans verify at human pace (days to months per claim)
- **Expertise Required:** Each domain requires specialist reviewers
- **Journal Capacity:** Peer review systems not designed for AI claim volume

**Scenarios:**
1. **Best Case:** Independent verification confirms breakthroughs, accelerating science
2. **Moderate Case:** Some claims validated, others refuted, signal-to-noise problem emerges
3. **Worst Case:** Verification backlog creates "pending claims" purgatory, undermining scientific trust

**The Neuron's observation ("peer-review inbox will need its own GPU cluster") captures the asymmetry: AI claim generation scales with compute, human verification doesn't.
