## 🔥 Key Highlights

### AT&T Operates 500 Agentic Workflows Processing 45B Tokens Daily
AT&T Corp. has deployed nearly 500 agentic AI workflows in production, processing approximately 45 billion AI tokens daily and handling over 1 million transactions monthly through its Business Agent Foundry platform. The telecom giant plans to triple its workflow count in 2027, with production AI investments achieving 5x returns within a year. AT&T's approach focuses on redesigning entire business processes rather than automating individual tasks, with autonomy granted only after accuracy reaches the mid-to-high 90% range. [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/14/att-uses-agentic-ai-to-reinvent-business-processes-from-the-ground-up/) | [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/att-runs-500-agentic-ai-workflows-processing-45b-tokens-a-day-targets-3x-by-2027)

### LangChain Raises $300M Series C at $3B Valuation
LangChain secured $300 million in Series C funding at a $3 billion valuation, led by Alphabet's CapitalG with participation from GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP, and Sapphire Ventures. This more than doubles the company's valuation from $1.25B a year ago. LangSmith, its framework-agnostic agent operations platform, now serves over 7,000 customers including Nvidia, Toyota, Salesforce, Harvey, Rippling, and Clay. CapitalG's Mo Jomaa stated: "The transition from experimental LLM applications to production-grade agents is one of the most significant shifts happening in enterprise software today." [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/langchain-300-million-series-c-funding-led-by-capitalg-at-3-billion-valuation-to-expand-ai-agent-platform/) | [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/langchain-hits-3b-valuation-on-300m-series-c-led-by-capitalg)

### Google Antigravity /teamwork Formalizes Major Mathematical Proofs
Google Research unveiled /teamwork, a multi-agent feature within its Antigravity platform that coordinates specialist subagents against open research problems. The system produced a fully machine-checked Lean formalization of the Weil conjectures in 1.8 million lines, a 919K-line formalization of Hilbert's tenth problem, and roughly 50 million Lean 4 lines for a Courtade–Kumar proof. Additional achievements include 95%/100% top-1/top-3 molecular structure identification from NMR and a Rust port of libgav1 20–30% faster than the C++ original. Google is opening these tools to external researchers. [Google Research](https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/) | [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/googles-antigravity-teamwork-multi-agent-system-formalizes-weil-hilberts-10th) | [Hacker News (177 pts)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50496811)

### FBI Issues ARTEX Framework Security Alert
The FBI issued advisory FLASH-20261113-001 warning that threat actors are weaponizing the ARTEX autonomous penetration testing framework, with intrusions progressing from initial targeting to privileged access in under three hours. One campaign stole personal data on approximately 67,000 individuals in South Korea, another breach compromised a ConnectWise Cloud Backup tenant affecting more than 2,250 downstream customers, and researchers identified over 1,600 publicly exposed ARTEX consoles by early November. Attackers predominantly route the Go-based framework through Chinese model APIs including DeepSeek, GLM, and Kimi alongside Claude and GPT. [GB Hackers](https://gbhackers.com/fbi-warns-artex-ai-framework/) | [Web Pro News](https://www.webpronews.com/fbi-flash-alert-artex-ai-agent-cyberattacks) | [The Record](https://therecord.media/artex-ai-tool-fbi-flash-alert)

### Sierra Releases Poppy 1.0 Personal Agent Protocol
Sierra released Personal Agent Protocol (Poppy) 1.0 on November 13, defining how a user's personal agent discovers a company and negotiates allowed actions through APIs, websites, or company agents. Every Shopify store in 175+ countries auto-publishes a Poppy file with no merchant setup required, and Meta's Muse agent now searches for Poppy on every site it visits by default. Over 60 additional design partners — including Cisco, Delta, Intuit, Twilio, and Zoom — joined earlier adopters, pushing total adoption past 100 companies at launch. [Sierra Blog](https://sierra.ai/blog/releasing-poppy) | [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/sierra-ships-poppy-10-personal-agent-protocol-as-shopify-auto-enables-it-and)

## 📚 Research Papers & Developments

### Google Gemini 4 Hydrogen Released for Coding Tasks
Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a new model designed specifically for everyday coding and multi-step agent tasks. The release aims to provide developers with enhanced capabilities for practical applications in software development and complex task execution. [AI Agents Directory](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-november-15-2026) | [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/editors-blog/in-the-wild-2026-11-15)

### Stanford Releases Memorilla Memory Module
Stanford researchers released Memorilla as open source on November 14, a 194M-parameter memory module that projects documents into just 16 latent tokens for a frozen Qwen3-8B. The team reports 84.33% accuracy on FactKG while using 108x fewer tokens than full-context prompting, 27x lower end-to-end latency, and 44.6% on PersonaMem-v2 against 30.9% for the next-best method. Code and training recipe are available under Apache 2.0. [emergent.sh](https://emergent.sh/news/stanford-open-sources-memorilla-memory-module) | [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

### Microsoft Lazarus Research Exposes Sleeper Agent Backdoors
Microsoft Research disclosed Lazarus on November 10, demonstrating how editing just seven dormant neurons can create AI model backdoors. The altered model can perform ordinary tasks while producing a chosen response when a specific trigger appears, exposing risks for AI assistants trusted with files and tools. Microsoft recommends weight-integrity checks and limited agent permissions, noting that ordinary task performance does not establish model safety. Paper submitted to arXiv on November 11. [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/microsoft-found-a-way-to-turn-a-helpful-ai-into-a-sleeper-agent/) | [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/microsoft-research-dormant-neuron-backdoor-ai-agents-2026)

### Institutional Design for Autonomous AI Agent Populations
A November 2026 arXiv paper argues that governing persistent autonomous AI agent populations requires institutional rules rather than model-level tuning. The research highlights the need for governance frameworks as AI agents become more autonomous and persistent. [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/institutional-design-autonomous-multi-agent-ai-populations-2026)

### Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small (594B Parameters)
Thinking Machines Lab published full weights for Inkling 2 Small on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 on November 12, following external safety and red-team testing. The MoE model has 594B total parameters with 17B active, supports image inputs and variable effort, and ships as a 393.5 GB checkpoint across 36 files. Together AI, Fireworks, Baseten, Nebius, Modal, and Cloudflare now offer inference. [Thinking Machines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-small/) | [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

## 🛠️ Tools & Frameworks

### IBM Granite 4.1 LLMON Reduces Prompt Injection
IBM released Granite 4.1 LLMON-preview models in 3B and 8B sizes on November 12, replacing conventional chat templates with LLMON — a markup that wraps every prompt block and tags each with execution permissions. Internal tests show indirect prompt-injection success dropping from 53-100% on baselines to 0.02-5.6% when the privilege rule treats only exec-authoritative instructions as commands. IBM cautions the behavior is learned, not enforced, and should not be the sole defense for agents with access to sensitive systems. [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/ibm-granite-41-llmon-cuts-indirect-prompt-injection-rates)

### NVIDIA Serves GLM-5.3 Across Two DGX Stations
NVIDIA's developer blog demonstrated serving GLM-5.3 NVFP4, with 753 billion parameters and 40 billion activated per token, across two GB300 DGX Stations using vLLM. At approximately 464 GB, the checkpoint exceeds the 252 GB of HBM3e on a single GB300 GPU. Tensor parallelism (TP=2) splits the model, with throughput peaking at 607.8 tokens per second at concurrency 16. [NVIDIA Developer Blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/when-one-gpu-isnt-enough-serving-glm-5-3-across-two-nvidia-dgx-stations-with-vllm/) | [Hacker News (130 pts)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50497155)

### browser-use 1.0.5 Adds AI Judgment Feature
browser-use released version 1.0.5 on November 14, introducing an AI judgment feature where "every run is judged by your own model." One additional request fills result.judgement with fields such as failure_reason, prompt_injection_detected, and gotchas. Across 13 runs, the vendor reports gotchas detected "on 15/15 verdicts where the site pushed back (6/15 before)." [GitHub](https://github.com/browser-use/browser-use/releases/tag/1.0.5) | [munderdiffl.in](https://munderdiffl.in/blog/agent-tools-today-2026-11-15/)

### Agweb: Browser Built for AI Agents
Agweb, a WebKit-based browser for macOS, launched as an agent-first browsing solution that "never takes focus." A dashboard shows every tab of every agent at once. Version 0.3.0, released November 14, prevents clicks on covered elements by naming the cover instead. The browser installs agent skills for Claude Code and Codex with a --secret-cmd feature for typing secrets agents never see in replies. Created November 11; MIT license, 189 stars. Requires macOS 26 or later. [GitHub](https://github.com/paul-nameless/agweb) | [Hacker News (91 pts)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50497155)

### OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet for Agent Purchases
OpenAI rolled out ChatGPT Wallet on November 13, allowing Dots agents to purchase items on behalf of users after one-time card entry and per-transaction approval. The feature uses network-issued payment tokens so agents never see raw card data, launches with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa as partners, and is initially limited to Pro subscribers aged 18+ in the US. OpenAI is not taking a transaction cut at launch while building merchant relationships. [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openais-chatgpt-wallet-lets-dots-agents-buy-for-us-pro-users)

## 🔒 Security & Safety

### Cursor Confirms Fireworks AI Key Leak Enabled Malicious Extension
Cursor confirmed that attackers used an access key stolen from Fireworks AI to publish two malicious copies of Anthropic's Claude Code extension (v2.1.312 and 2.1.313) on Cursor's marketplace on October 29, live for about 11 hours. Up to 5,000 developers may have downloaded the trojanized builds, which attempted to install a persistent SSH backdoor and exfiltrate system info. Fireworks-hosted historical coding-agent traces containing unredacted credentials were also accessed. Cursor rotated keys and moved all inference off Fireworks. [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/cursor-confirms-fireworks-leak-enabled-malicious-claude-code)

### Lovable Apps Exposed Private Data Including Passports
Security firm Crissy Field examined 159,041 Lovable projects and found 24,664 sites with Supabase databases readable without authentication. The exposure includes more than 200,000 private chat messages, 80,000+ user profiles, over 1,000 passport records with numbers and nationalities, and 280,000 customer emails. Researchers trace the pattern to Lovable's uniform code-gen scaffolding leaving frontend API keys with unrestricted database permissions. [Crissy Field Research](https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/) | [Hacker News (699 pts)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50499017)

### DOJ Disrupts North Korean FaceStealer Deepfake Operation
US authorities seized the cloud infrastructure behind FaceStealer, an 18-month North Korean social-engineering campaign attributed to BlueNoroff that netted over $400M in crypto. The scheme lured victims into fake Zoom/Teams calls that replayed prerecorded webcam footage of trusted contacts to simulate their presence. The FBI recovered 900+ secretly recorded webcam clips; Arctic Wolf identified 677 targets in 58 countries, over 80% founders or C-suite. Justice also froze $2.7B tied to a $6.4B Binance heist. [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/doj-disrupts-north-koreas-facestealer-deepfake-call-scheme-after-400m-crypto)

## 💡 Insights & Analysis

### Enterprise AI Agent Adoption Accelerates
AT&T's deployment of 500 agentic workflows processing 45 billion tokens daily represents a significant milestone in enterprise AI adoption. The company's approach of redesigning entire business processes rather than automating individual tasks, combined with 5x ROI within a year, validates the business case for large-scale agent deployment. The requirement for mid-to-high 90% accuracy before granting autonomy demonstrates a mature governance framework that other enterprises can learn from.

### Agent Security Becomes Critical Priority
This week highlighted the dual nature of AI agents' security challenges. On the offensive side, the FBI's ARTEX warning demonstrates how agents can compress attack timelines from days to hours, while the North Korean FaceStealer operation shows sophisticated social engineering at scale. On the defensive side, the Cursor/Fireworks breach, Lovable data exposures, and Microsoft's Lazarus research all point to the need for robust security frameworks including weight integrity checks, privilege controls, and proper secret management.

### From Experimental to Production-Grade Agents
LangChain's $300M Series C at a $3B valuation, with 7,000+ enterprise customers, signals the market's transition from experimental LLM applications to production-grade agent infrastructure. CapitalG's description of this as "one of the most significant shifts happening in enterprise software today" aligns with AT&T's operational experience and validates the investment in agent orchestration platforms.

### Multi-Agent Systems Tackle Grand Challenges
Google's Antigravity /teamwork represents a breakthrough in applying multi-agent systems to fundamental research problems. The formalization of the Weil conjectures (1.8M lines), Hilbert's tenth problem (919K lines), and other mathematical proofs demonstrates that coordinated specialist agents can tackle problems previously requiring deep human expertise. This pattern of decomposing complex tasks across specialized agents may become a dominant paradigm in AI-assisted research.

### Interoperability Through Standards
Sierra's Poppy protocol, instantly live on every Shopify store and integrated into Meta's Muse agent by default, shows how standards can accelerate agent adoption. With 100+ companies already participating, Poppy could become the HTTP of agent-to-business interactions, enabling a new ecosystem of personal AI assistants that seamlessly transact across services.

### The Memory Challenge Gets Solved
Stanford's Memorilla demonstrates a path forward for long-context challenges, compressing documents to just 16 tokens with 108x token reduction while maintaining 84% accuracy. This addresses one of the key bottlenecks in production agent systems — the cost and latency of processing large context windows. Similar compression techniques may become standard in enterprise agent deployments.
