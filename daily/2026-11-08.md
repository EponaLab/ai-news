## 📚 Research & Analysis

### MIT Technology Review: Three Critical Agent Governance Papers

MIT Technology Review published three significant pieces addressing fundamental challenges in agentic enterprise systems:

**1. Securing the Agentic Enterprise with Data Lineage**

Examines the accountability problem when agents pull data from multiple sources without human verification at each handoff. Scenario: Sales agent pulls from restricted pricing, internal comms, and archived quotes → second agent uses unverified data to draft proposals.

**Key insight:** Traditional access controls insuffi cient for multi-agent workflows. Data lineage tracking becomes essential infrastructure for audit trails in agent-driven enterprises.

[Source: MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/04/1147596/securing-the-agentic-enterprise-with-data-lineage/)

**2. How "Reverse Game Theory" Could Keep AI Agents in Check**

Introduces "reverse game theory" (mechanism design) as alignment approach: designing systems that incentivize agents to stick to rules rather than relying on model-level constraints.

**Approach:** Rather than training agents to follow rules, design environments where rule-following behavior is the economically rational strategy.

**Why now:** As agents become more capable and long-running, prompt-based constraints prove insufficient. Mechanism design offers structural alternative.

[Source: MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/03/1147934/reverse-game-theory-mechanism-design-ai-alignment/)

**3. Raising the Quality of Insights in Agentic Analytics**

Addresses the reliability problem in agent-generated business insights. While generative AI democratized analytics access, agentic AI promises end-to-end workflow automation—but current insight quality remains problematic.

**Challenge:** Agents making decisions based on their own insights without quality verification creates compounding error risk.

**Need:** Standardized evaluation frameworks for agent-generated analytical outputs before enterprises can safely delegate decision-making.

[Source: MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/02/1147660/raising-the-quality-of-insights-in-agentic-analytics/)
